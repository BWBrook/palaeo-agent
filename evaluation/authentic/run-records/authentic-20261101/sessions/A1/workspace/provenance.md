# Coed Duon shelly fauna: provenance and stratigraphy

**Source.** Specimen counts from the Coed Duon section, Upper Llanvirn
(Middle Ordovician), Wales, from the benthic palaeocommunity study of
Williams, Lockley & Hurst (1981). The file is the copy distributed with the
PAST case studies (Hammer, Harper & Ryan 2001, *Palaeontologia Electronica*
4(1)), case study 8.

**Stratigraphy.**
- Ten horizons, numbered upwards: horizon 7 overlies horizon 6.
- Horizons 1–6 lie in the Upper Flags and Grits.
- Horizons 7–10 lie in the overlying Ashes and Lavas, separated from the
  Upper Flags and Grits by an unconformity between horizons 6 and 7.
- No dates, thicknesses or spacings between horizons are available.

**File `llanvirn.dat`.**
- Whitespace-separated. The first row holds the horizon numbers (1–10); the
  `.` at its start is a placeholder for the corner cell.
- Each further row is one taxon: a label, then its specimen count at each
  horizon.
- Labels are five-letter abbreviations of genus names, such as `Sower`
  (*Sowerbyella*) and `Dalma` (*Dalmanella*).
- Each label begins with a code of the form `_N_` carried from the archived
  file. The archive does not document what the code means.

**Not recorded.** Sampling effort per horizon (rock volume, area or collecting
time), how specimens were counted (for example, how disarticulated valves
were treated), and any replicate collections.
