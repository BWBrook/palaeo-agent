# Tasman slope core-top foraminifera

Sediment was washed on a 63 µm sieve and the residue dry-sieved at 150 µm into two fractions, picked separately.

- `counts_gt150.tsv`: tab-separated; taxa as rows, cores as columns; specimens picked from the >150 µm fraction.
- `counts_63_150.tsv`: the same layout for the 63–150 µm fraction.
- `picking.csv`: one row per core and fraction: `status` and `picker`.
- `cores.csv`: `core_id`, `campaign`, position.
- `campaigns.csv`: `vessel` and `sieve_mesh_um` per campaign.
