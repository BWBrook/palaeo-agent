# Llanvirn trilobite and brachiopod counts

`counts.tsv` is a classic PAST table: the first line lists core codes, and each later line gives one taxon's counts across the cores.

`field_log.csv` records, for each core, the depositional zone assigned in the field (A: inner shelf, B: outer shelf) and the water depth estimated from sedimentology. The log lists cores in the order they were described, not in core-code order.

Core codes in the field log were written without zero padding.
