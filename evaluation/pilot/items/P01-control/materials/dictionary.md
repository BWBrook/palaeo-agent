# Hirnantian brachiopod counts

`counts.tsv` is a classic PAST table: the first line lists core numbers, and each later line gives one taxon's specimen counts in those cores. Taxon codes are five-letter abbreviations of genera.

Cores were taken at 1 m spacing through one section; core numbers increase upwards.

All cores were picked and counted in full by one analyst in 2019.

Counts are whole specimens; fragments were not counted.
