# Atlantis Bank planktonic foraminifera, two counting laboratories

- `counts.tsv`: tab-separated; one row per sample, one column per name as recorded by the counting laboratory.
- `taxonomy.csv`: every recorded name with its `accepted_name` under current taxonomy.
- `samples.csv`: `sample_id`, `counting_lab`, `water_depth_m`.
- `lab_log.csv`: processing log.
