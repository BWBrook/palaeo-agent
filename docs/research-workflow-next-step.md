## Decision after recalibration

Keep the prespecified A/B/halt classification and all outcomes. Do not change
the scoring, definitions or thresholds after seeing results. Separate that
classification from whether more trials are worth running:

- If direct analysis is again at ceiling, stop seeking correctness headroom
  in this bank. Do not build a harder synthetic bank to obtain an effect.
- Inspect final, adjudicated replay, package losses, toolkit adherence,
  scientific failures, token cost and the sources of friction. Calibration
  traps alone do not estimate control harm or reviewer performance.
- Run further formal trials only if their result can change a named
  development decision. A ceiling does not itself justify automatically
  spending on the 24-control/48-reviewer branch. A persistent package or
  auditability question might justify that branch; the evidence must say why.
- A discriminating recalibration makes branch A feasible, but eight direct
  trials do not establish a toolkit benefit or guarantee an informative pilot.
  Retain uncertainty and distinguish package-contract failures from scientific
  errors in interpreting the routing result.

The live runner remains Claude under the agreed division of work. Finish
the authorised ≤16 trials/≤400,000 counted tokens after final qualification,
with no automatic retry or extra round. Return the outcome and a concrete
recommendation before launching the larger study. The existing safeguards
and reviewer-package qualification requirements remain in force.

## A bounded authentic complement

Candidate: the Coed Duon Upper Llanvirn shelly-fauna data, already archived
with source provenance as `benchmarks/past/legacy-data/llanvirn.dat` and
`benchmarks/past/vignettes/html/study8.html`. The source describes ten horizons
and the transitions between 5–6 and 6–7, with an unconformity at the latter.
This is a genuine fossil dataset represented in a historical teaching subset.
It is familiar to the developers and has existing toolkit benchmarks; it
cannot serve as an untouched discovery dataset or evidence about advanced
statistical complexity. A better documented real dataset already available
to Claude can replace it without starting a new acquisition programme.

Proposed research brief:

> How does assemblage composition change through the Coed Duon succession,
> particularly between horizons 5–6 and 6–7? Which interpretations remain
> supported after considering differences in counting totals and defensible
> ways of representing abundance? Produce a checkable analysis and explain
> what the sampling and stratigraphic record cannot establish.

Provide the original counts, a factual provenance and stratigraphic note,
and the current public toolkit documentation. Keep the tutorial recipe,
suggested answers, developer benchmarks and previous analyses outside the
agent's workspace. Do not plant errors, prescribe a sequence of methods, or
require the toolkit. Supply ordinary scientific libraries alongside it and
allow the agent to choose its approach. Declining a method or the toolkit can
be a reasonable outcome. Do not infer replicated temporal effects from one
section or invent sampling effort, dates or independent replicates.

Start with one fresh agent session. If a direct comparison would resolve a
specific question, use one separate fresh session with the identical brief,
data, libraries, resource ceiling and runnable-deliverable requirement, but
without the toolkit. Fix the model/version and these conditions first. Keep
sessions isolated; do not give one the other's analysis. Any such pair is
descriptive evidence, not an estimate of average treatment benefit.

Observe adoption without prompting the agent to justify every tool choice
during the work. Retain traces, then ask a short neutral retrospective about
selection or non-selection. Separate that self-report from observed actions.
Record:

- whether and where the agent discovers, selects, abandons or bypasses the API;
- source-to-result transformations, data or method refusals and workarounds;
- unsupported scientific needs, distinct from missing documentation;
- time and tokens, including preparation, discovery and repair;
- a clean replay from the original inputs and saved executable analysis;
- whether an independent reviewer can trace the main claims and identify
  unsupported inferences from the delivered package.

Use ordinary scripts and the existing confined execution/replay machinery.
Require a runnable entry point and saved settings in both sessions. No new
reviewer-flaw experiment or scoring bank is needed for this observation.
Judge the next capability from a demonstrated research need that would recur,
not from whichever operation would make the toolkit arm win. Possible
decisions include improving documentation, simplifying the API, fixing a
specific reproducibility problem, adding one scientifically justified method,
or leaving the current capability unchanged.

Claude's response and the recalibration outcome will determine the practical
recommendation. No authentic-workflow trial has been launched by this note.
